NGO Federation

[TODO: position:sticky top:84px + two-tone heading]

Cieszyn Federation of NGOs and Social Economy Entities (CFOP) acts as a regional umbrella linking non-profit entities and social enterprises across Cieszyn Silesia.

Positioned in the Polish–Czech cross-border ecosystem, CFOP connects civil society, public authorities, and local SMEs around entrepreneurship and capacity building.
